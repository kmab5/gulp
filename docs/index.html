06. good to know

A few
questions.

Short answers. You can find the implementation and source code on GitHub.

Does gulp need an account? +

No. Gulp is designed to work offline, with drink records saved on your Android device.

Does the water show my hydration level? +

No. The water is a visual representation of recorded drinks relative to a display reference. It does not measure your physiological hydration or tell you how much to drink.

Can I turn reminders or animations off? +

Yes. Reminders are optional, and you can adjust water motion, touch effects, and reduced-motion settings.

Can I change or remove entries? +

Yes. You can edit older entries, delete individual records, export a CSV, or delete your saved drink history.

Where can I get the Android app? +

Check the GitHub releases page for published builds. If no installable release is available yet, the source repository includes Android build instructions.
