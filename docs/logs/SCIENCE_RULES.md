# Hydration and safety rules — future-phase implementation contract

- This is a *recording and prompting* app, not a diagnostic hydration sensor.
- Never turn logged fraction or an illustration's water level into a medical hydration percentage.
- Allow goal-free reminders and user-chosen tracking references. Never force universal 8-glass/2-litre requirements.
- Regular drinks count as ordinary approximate beverage volume; don't invent correction factors for coffee or tea.
- Missed reminders create no debt; 'not thirsty' is a valid choice; no aggressive catch-up, overdrinking reward or punitive streak.
- No alerts during quiet hours; suppression after recently logged drinks; notifications need user consent on recent Android versions.
- A missing day is 'no entries', not 'no water drunk'. Reports describe only *recorded* actions.
- Certain medical situations require individualized advice; users on clinician-prescribed fluid management must not get automatic generic volume targets.
- Adult-first scope; clear source provenance and clinical review before specific medical claims.

See archived source science research in `reference/evidence/` for the supporting references and scope limitations. The app requests no health information or health permissions in Phase 1.
